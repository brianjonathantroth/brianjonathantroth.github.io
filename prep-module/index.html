Writing sample 1 · HIV prevention e-learning

Closing the PrEP Knowledge Gap

A competency-based e-learning module that helps HIV testing and intake staff ask the right questions, recognize who could benefit from PrEP, and hand clients off to care. Learners practice in two branching client conversations and see how each choice affects client trust.

Audience: HIV testing and intake staffAbout 15 minutesBranching scenariosKnowledge checkPractice aidSection 508 conscious

Learning objectives

By the end, learners will be able to:

  • Open a sexual health conversation with a universal, permission-based statement.
  • Ask about partners and practices using neutral, behavior-based language.
  • Identify at least two intake details that suggest a client could benefit from PrEP.
  • Describe the current PrEP options in plain language, including twice-yearly injections.
  • Close with a warm handoff to testing or PrEP navigation.

What is inside

  • Two branching scenarios with clients Marcus and Amina. Each choice gets written feedback, and a client trust meter shows how the conversation is going.
  • A plain-language PrEP options guide covering daily pills, every-two-month injections, and twice-yearly lenacapavir.
  • A knowledge check whose wrong answers are the real mistakes from the scenarios, so feedback points back to a specific moment.
  • A printable practice aid for the intake desk.

Design choices

The module replaces "risk" with questions about specific practices and protection, because risk means different things to different people. Feedback explains the why behind each recommendation, the same way clients deserve to hear the why behind theirs.

Accessibility: keyboard navigation, screen reader announcements for feedback, alt text on every image, reduced-motion support, and light and dark themes.

How I made this

I wrote and revised the scenarios, dialogue, and feedback, chose every photo, and checked each clinical claim against current CDC guidance. I used AI tools (Claude) for production support: coding the interactive module and checking my drafts against sources.

Key source: Patel RR, et al. Updated CDC guidance on lenacapavir for HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis. MMWR 2025;74(35):541-549. Full references are in the module’s References section.
