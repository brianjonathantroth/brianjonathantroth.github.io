Writing sample 2 · Video script for clinicians

Twice-Yearly PrEP: What Changes at the Visit

A two-minute narrated briefing that introduces clinicians to twice-yearly injectable lenacapavir: who it is for, the HIV testing required before every injection, how dosing works, and a simple way to start the conversation with patients.

Audience: clinicians2.5 minutesTwo-column scriptCaptioned358-word narration

What the script covers

  • Why it matters: a daily pill means 365 chances a year for life to get in the way, and many primary care providers do not start PrEP conversations.
  • Evidence: PURPOSE 1 and PURPOSE 2 results in cisgender women, cisgender men, and gender-diverse people.
  • Eligibility and testing: adults and adolescents weighing at least 35 kg; lab-based HIV testing before every injection.
  • Dosing: two injections and oral tablets to start, then injections every six months.
  • Call to action: one plain sentence clinicians can say to their next patient.

Design choices

The script is written in the two-column format used in production: narration on the left and on-screen text and visuals on the right, so a designer or animator can build from it directly. Every scene is captioned for viewers watching without sound.

How I made this

I wrote the narration and revised it over several drafts, and checked every clinical claim against current CDC guidance. I used AI tools (Claude) to help animate and render the video and to check my drafts against sources. The narration is an AI voice generated in NaturalReader.

Key source: Patel RR, et al. Updated CDC guidance on lenacapavir for HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis. MMWR 2025;74(35):541-549. Full references are in the script.
